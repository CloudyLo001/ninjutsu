Hand signs

Paper 開

One hand open flat, fingers spread — on your right hand.

Rasenshuriken on your palm
Chidori 千鳥

The same flat palm, on your left hand, held any way up.

Lightning gathers in your hand
Cross 交

Index and middle out on both hands, fingers crossing partway down.

Two shadow clones beside you
Ram 未

Same fingers, angled into a steeple — fingertips touching.

Log substitution, you vanish 5s

Paper and Chidori are the same shape on different hands; cross and ram are the same fingers, and the difference is where they meet — crossed partway down, or touching at the tips.

Rasenshuriken

Open one hand flat — the paper sign — and a Rasenshuriken forms on your palm. Tilt your hand to angle it.
The same flat palm on your left hand gathers a Chidori instead.
Cross two fingers on both hands and two shadow clones appear beside you. Release and they go up in smoke.
Ram seal — same two fingers, angled into a steeple with the fingertips touching — swaps you for a log and you vanish for five seconds.
